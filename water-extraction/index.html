Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. Quick removal of standing water means less harm to your flooring, your belongings, the building's systems and the structure itself. With each hour it sits, water works farther in: through carpet to the pad, up the drywall, into wood framing and down to the subfloor. For emergency water extraction in Oshkosh, Oshkosh Restoration brings truck-mounted units and portable extractors built to pull out hundreds of gallons every minute.

Few water removal systems match the power of a truck-mounted extractor, which draws water from structural materials, carpeting and hard flooring far better than rental or consumer machines. Where the truck hoses will not reach, we bring in submersible pumps, strong portable extractors and specialty tools made for water caught between floor layers, inside wall cavities or in tight spaces.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. The more moisture our specialty tools take from these concealed areas ahead of drying, the shorter and lighter the structural drying job becomes, and the lower the chance of secondary mold growth.

Warning Signs for Water Extraction

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Saturated carpet that squishes underfoot or shows water pooling
  • Active leaks from plumbing, appliances or HVAC systems
  • Water visible in crawl spaces, mechanical rooms or utility areas
  • Water flowing or dripping from ceilings, walls or fixtures
  • Water entering through a flooded basement stairwell or window well
  • Water accumulating after a fire suppression system discharges
  • Any room where water has collected on the floor

Water Extraction Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and label it clearly
  • Check the supply hoses on your appliances and swap out any that look old or worn
  • Shelving in low areas keeps belongings out of the water if the floor floods
  • Program (708) 493-8727 into your phone so you can call immediately in an emergency
  • Regularly inspect under sinks, around toilets and near appliances for early signs of a leak
  • Install automatic water shutoff valves that detect leaks and stop the flow
