When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Oshkosh

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Several hours or more have passed with hardwood, drywall or carpet sitting wet.
  • A growing pool on the floor is heading for the next room or running downstairs.
  • Water is coming through a ceiling, light fixture or electrical outlet.
  • The water keeps running, and the shutoff valve is out of reach or nowhere to be found.
  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.
  • Part of your ceiling bows downward because water is pooling on top of it.
  • A water heater tank has failed and is emptying across the floor.

Maintenance Tips for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Put leak alarms beneath sinks and beside the dishwasher, washer and water heater.
  • In basements and other low areas, keep documents and valuables up on shelves.
  • Any trip beyond a few days is a reason to turn the main supply off before you go.
  • Locate the main shutoff valve now, and show each person at home how to turn it off.
  • Replace old supply hoses on washers, toilets and faucets before they burst.
  • Know where your electrical panel is so power to wet rooms can be cut safely.
